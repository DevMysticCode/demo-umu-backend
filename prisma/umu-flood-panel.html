What this means when you buy

  • Insurance. Properties built before 2009 in a flood-risk area are usually eligible for Flood Re, which caps premiums. Newer builds are not.
  • Mortgage. Some lenders decline or require a specialist survey where river and sea risk is High.
  • Ask your conveyancer for a full flood report and the seller's answers on past flooding in the property information form.
